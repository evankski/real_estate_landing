Information and professional advice

Property, market, cost, neighborhood, commute, and other information can change. Confirm the information relevant to your decision through current records, disclosures, direct sources, and appropriate professionals. General examples on this website are not an appraisal, property inspection, financing offer, insurance quote, or guarantee of an outcome.

This website does not provide legal, tax, lending, insurance, or investment advice. Consult the appropriately licensed professional for advice in those areas.

Inquiries and representation

The website inquiry form requests a conversation. It is not a buyer-broker representation agreement, a listing agreement, or an offer to purchase a property. We will discuss the scope of services, representation, timing, and compensation and complete the appropriate written agreements when required.

Real estate commissions are not set by law and are negotiable. The applicable written agreement will explain compensation and when it is due. Do not assume that a seller will pay a buyer’s broker or that services are free; discuss the arrangements for your transaction before proceeding. The California Department of Real Estate provides information about buyer representation and compensation.

This website is not intended to solicit buyers or sellers currently subject to an exclusive representation agreement with another broker. If you are exclusively represented, direct transaction-specific requests to your current agent.
