Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Who this notice covers

This notice describes information collected through this website by Evan Karwowski, a California real estate salesperson affiliated with WR Properties, a registered DBA of Winning Results Realty, Inc. You can contact Evan at evan@thewowski.com or 925-492-7023.

Information you choose to provide

An inquiry may include your name, email address, optional phone number, whether you are buying or selling, preferred area, budget range, timeline, contact preference, and any listing link or message you provide. We also receive your response-consent selection, the consent text version, a submission timestamp, and your optional tracking preference.

The form does not ask for income, Social Security numbers, bank details, or credit documents. Please do not include sensitive financial or identity information in your message.

Where your inquiry goes

When the website’s contact-management connection is configured, inquiries are sent to Follow Up Boss so Evan can manage the contact and follow up on your request. This can include your contact details, housing preferences, qualification answers, message, campaign information, and consent records.

When the email route is used, FormSubmit processes the submission and forwards it to Evan’s email. FormSubmit and email service providers handle the information needed to deliver and manage that message. See FormSubmit’s privacy terms for its practices.

We use your inquiry to respond, discuss the service you requested, and keep relevant correspondence and transaction records. Submitting the form does not subscribe you to a newsletter or authorize unrelated marketing.

Website and campaign information

A submission can include the page path, the referring website’s origin, and campaign labels present in the page URL, such as UTM source, medium, campaign, content, and term. These labels help us understand which page or advertisement led to an inquiry. They are submitted with the form; the site does not store your form answers in browser storage.

Hosting, form, contact-management, and email providers may process technical information such as an IP address, request information, and browser details when operating their services.

Optional advertising measurement

With your permission, the site loads Meta Pixel to measure page visits and successful inquiry events. Meta may receive event, page, browser, device, and network information through that tool. The site does not intentionally send your name, email, phone number, budget, or message as Pixel event parameters.

Optional tracking remains off until you choose to allow it. A browser’s Global Privacy Control signal is treated as an opt-out. You can use Cookie Settings or Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information in the footer to turn optional tracking off. See the Cookie Policy for details.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your inquiry contact details. Information may be handled by service providers supporting the website, forms, contact management, email, and advertising measurement, and by the responsible brokerage as needed to respond or provide real estate services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights. Optional advertising measurement can involve sharing information with Meta, which is why this site provides a tracking choice.

Inquiry and correspondence records are retained as needed to respond, provide services, and meet applicable brokerage, transaction, and legal recordkeeping obligations. Browser storage used by the site contains privacy preferences and temporary submission or measurement markers, not your form answers.

Your choices and requests

To ask about access, correction, deletion, or use of information you provided, email evan@thewowski.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line. We may need to confirm your identity to respond. Available rights and any exceptions depend on the laws that apply to the request.

You can contact Evan directly without completing the web form and can ask to stop follow-up communications. Privacy questions may also be mailed to WR Properties, 613 1st Street, Suite 211, Brentwood, CA 94513.

Other websites and updates

Links to social platforms and other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. This notice may be updated when this website’s practices change; the date above identifies the current version.
