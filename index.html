A few things you may be wondering

Before we talk.

Do I need to be ready to make a move?

No. An early conversation can help you narrow down the areas, price range, and timing that make sense for you. You can reach out while you’re still researching.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

I’ll review what you share and reach out using your preferred contact method. We’ll talk about your priorities, answer initial questions, and decide whether a home comparison, pricing report, or consultation is the best next step.

Can I send you homes I’m already considering?

Absolutely. Include a listing link in the form, or send it by email or text. Tell me what you like about it and what you’re unsure about. That gives us a useful place to start.

What if I need to sell and buy?

We’ll look at both sides together: likely sale proceeds, preparation, financing questions for your lender, and the timing of your next purchase. The plan should account for the whole move.

How does agent compensation work?

Real estate commissions are negotiable and are not set by law. We’ll discuss the amount, services, and who is responsible for payment in the applicable written agreement. A seller’s payment of a buyer’s agent is not assumed.

Does an inquiry commit me to working with you?

No. Sending this form requests a conversation. Before touring homes together, we’ll discuss the services, duration, and negotiable compensation in a written buyer representation agreement.
