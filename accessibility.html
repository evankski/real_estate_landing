Accessibility

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Using this website

I want the information on this website to be accessible to people with different abilities and ways of browsing. The site is designed with semantic headings, labeled form fields, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, meaningful image descriptions, and support for reduced motion preferences.

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA as a reference for reviewing and improving the site. Accessibility work includes checking how the site behaves with a keyboard, different screen sizes, and enlarged text, as well as addressing reported barriers.

Help with information or an inquiry

If something prevents you from using the website, please contact me at evan@thewowski.com or 925-492-7023. You can ask your real estate question directly without using the form.

When reporting an issue, include the page, what you were trying to do, and your preferred way to receive the information or a response. If you are comfortable sharing them, your browser and assistive technology can help identify the problem. You do not need to provide medical information. I will work with you to provide the information in an accessible format and address the issue.

Continued improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. Third-party websites and services linked from this site have their own accessibility features and limitations. If one creates a barrier, contact me for another way to continue.
