A closer look

What I would look at with you.

Start with the property’s full cost.

Compare the purchase price with the current tax bill, any special assessments, HOA dues, insurance quotes, and solar agreement. A similar asking price does not always mean a similar monthly cost. A purchase can trigger reassessment and supplemental property taxes, so the seller’s current bill does not establish your future tax cost. Confirm estimates with the county assessor and your tax adviser.

Look closely at the home and the lot.

Review the roof, heating and cooling, drainage, and any additions or converted spaces. For a newer home, ask about builder warranties, completed inspections, and landscaping that still needs to be done.

Try the location before deciding.

Drive your likely commute at the times you would use it. Visit the street again, check parking, and work out the distance to the places you visit regularly.

Questions to bring to a showing

  • Is the solar system owned, financed, or leased, and what transfers to the buyer?
  • What special taxes or assessments apply to this parcel?
  • Which repairs, permits, or builder documents need a closer look?
  • How would this home compare with an option in Oakley or Discovery Bay?
