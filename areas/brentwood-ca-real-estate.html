Buying or selling a home in Brentwood, CA

I grew up in Brentwood, in eastern Contra Costa County. When two homes here make your shortlist, I’d start with the bills for each address and the trips you’ll make from the front door.

Compare Brentwood homes

By Evan Karwowski · WR Properties · Updated November 3, 2026

Two Brentwood addresses, two sets of costs

Read the bills side by side

Pull each parcel’s secured tax bill and separate the amount based on assessed value from any special taxes and assessments. The current owner’s bill can reflect a much earlier purchase. Compare the parcel charges first, then use the county’s supplemental tax information to see how a change of ownership can affect what you owe. Contra Costa tax bill lookup · County supplemental tax guide

Check the school and the way there

If a particular school matters to your move, enter each street address in the Brentwood Union School District boundary tool and confirm the assignment with the district before relying on it. Check the high school separately with the district serving that grade. Brentwood Union attendance boundaries

Try the trail from the front door

Marsh Creek Regional Trail runs from Creekside Park in Brentwood toward Big Break. A listing might look close to the trail on a map while the nearest entrance is several turns away. If that ride or walk is part of the appeal, try it from the house. Marsh Creek Regional Trail map

Compare two Brentwood property tax bills

Use this walkthrough to find the special taxes and assessments on each bill and keep them separate from the seller’s assessed value.

What would your Brentwood home sell for?

For a downtown property, I’d look for sales with comparable lot use, parking and documented living space. For a newer subdivision home, I’d start with a similar floor plan and check upgrades, lot position and any seller credits. Send your address for the closed sales I’d use and the ones I’d leave out.
