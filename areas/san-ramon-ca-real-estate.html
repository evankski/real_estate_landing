San Ramon, CA real estate guide

When two San Ramon homes have similar prices, compare their parcel bills as well as their HOA dues. In parts of the city, an annual district assessment belongs in that calculation.

Plan your San Ramon home search

By Evan Karwowski · WR Properties

Does the Dougherty Valley assessment apply?

Locate the parcel on the district map.

The Dougherty Valley Geologic Hazard Abatement District includes areas of West Branch, Dougherty Valley and Old Ranch Summit. Properties inside its boundaries pay an annual assessment. Before comparing two listings, check each address against the district map and review the applicable parcel bill. The City also publishes the district’s engineer’s reports. San Ramon district boundaries and reports.

Separate the district’s work from the owner’s.

For a home next to open space, ask who maintains the slope, drains and retaining structures at the property line. Read the district’s plan of control alongside any HOA documents and inspection findings. If an answer affects repair costs, get it in writing from the district, association or an appropriate specialist.

Selling a home in San Ramon?

Before pricing a San Ramon home in the district, collect the current parcel assessment and HOA statement. I’d use those figures to show how your home compares with nearby sales, even when two houses with similar floor plans have different monthly costs.

Discuss your San Ramon sale

Every property is different. Confirm costs, condition, boundaries, school assignments, and permitted uses with the appropriate sources and professionals.
