Buying or selling a home in Discovery Bay

If you’re bringing a boat, the first question I’d ask at a showing is where you can take it from this dock. A water view, a usable berth and a route to the Delta answer three different needs.

Compare Discovery Bay homes

By Evan Karwowski · WR Properties · Updated November 3, 2026

What comes with the waterfront?

Trace the trip from the dock

Ask the seller to identify the route to open water, the dimensions of the berth and the depth at the dock at low water. Bring your boat’s length, beam and draft. Request the approval and maintenance records for the dock, then check with a qualified marine inspector if the condition or fit is unclear. For a lakefront home, confirm permitted uses with the association.

Know which office handles the work

Discovery Bay is an unincorporated community. The Town provides services including water and wastewater. Contra Costa County handles land use. The Town also describes a sanitation review for certain property improvements. If a deck or pool is part of your plans, check both the county permit history and the Town’s requirements for the parcel. Town of Discovery Bay services · Town sanitation approvals

Look up the parcel and get an insurance quote

Start with the address on FEMA’s flood map, whether the listing faces water or sits inland. Share the map and the property details with your insurance professional and lender to confirm coverage options, cost and any loan requirements for that specific home. FEMA Flood Map Service Center

Pricing a Discovery Bay property

For a waterfront sale, I’d start with sales offering similar water access, frontage, berth size and dock condition. Repair records and dock approvals help explain differences to a buyer. For an inland property, I’d look at homes competing for the same buyer before giving any weight to a waterfront sale.
