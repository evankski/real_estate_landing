Danville, CA real estate guide

A home backing onto the Iron Horse Trail and a home a few blocks from a trail entrance offer different routines. In Danville I’d walk that route with you and look just as closely at what’s been changed inside the house.

Plan your Danville home search

By Evan Karwowski · WR Properties

Which Danville features will you use?

Find the public trail entrance.

If you picture walking or biking into town, start at the front door and trace the route to a public entrance. Note the crossings and how you would get a bike out of the garage. For a home adjoining the trail, visit the back patio when you would normally use it and listen for yourself. East Bay Regional Park District trail map and access points.

Look up the addition by street address.

Suppose the house has a newer primary suite or a pool. Danville’s public permit search lets you enter the house number and street without an account. Match the project description and inspection record to the seller’s paperwork. Then ask the Town about any question that could change what you will pay. Town of Danville permit history.

Selling a home in Danville?

For a remodeled Danville home, gather plans, permit numbers and final inspection records. If trail access features in the listing, identify the public entrance buyers can use. Those details help us select comparable sales and describe what the next owner is buying.

Discuss your Danville sale

Every property is different. Confirm costs, condition, boundaries, school assignments, and permitted uses with the appropriate sources and professionals.
