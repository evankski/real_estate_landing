A closer look

What I would look at with you.

Check the lot as well as the house.

Review drainage, slopes, retaining structures, trees, and outdoor improvements. Ask specialists to assess findings that affect maintenance or future plans.

Price the ongoing responsibilities.

Get insurance information and review taxes, HOA obligations, and the condition of major systems. Consider the maintenance of pools, landscaping, or other features if present.

Verify plans for the property.

If you want to expand or change the home, check permits, zoning, setbacks, and any HOA restrictions before relying on that possibility.

Questions to bring to a showing

  • What would the home require you to maintain each year?
  • Are your intended improvements permitted under the applicable rules?
  • Which features are worth paying for in your own use of the home?
