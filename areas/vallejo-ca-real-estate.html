Vallejo, CA real estate guide

If you’re considering Vallejo because of the ferry to San Francisco, work backward from the time you need to arrive. A short distance to the waterfront can still mean a drive, a parking stop and a walk to the boat.

Plan your Vallejo home search

By Evan Karwowski · WR Properties

Would the Vallejo ferry work for your week?

Try the commute in both directions.

Start at the home, route yourself to the Vallejo terminal on Mare Island Way, and allow time to reach the boarding area. For the return, pick a sailing that works after a late meeting and identify a backup. Compare that door-to-door trip with your other commute options before deciding how much terminal access is worth to you. San Francisco Bay Ferry’s Vallejo terminal and connections.

Price parking for the days you travel.

Downtown has designated paid lots and hourly, daily and longer-term options. Locate the lot you would use, check its rules and compare the cost for your actual office days with the cost of walking or taking transit. If you’re buying downtown, separately confirm any parking that comes with the residence. City of Vallejo downtown parking map and fees.

Selling a home in Vallejo?

For a Vallejo listing marketed to ferry riders, I’d verify the route from the house, identify any assigned or deeded parking and compare homes with a similar trip. Buyers can test the route from your front door themselves.
