East Bay and Solano real estate area guides

Which homes deserve a second look? Start with a city to see what I’d check before a showing or a listing appointment, from the cost of owning the home to the trip you’d make every morning.

Where are you looking?

I grew up in Brentwood. When two listings have similar asking prices, their tax bills can still look quite different. Here’s how I compare two Brentwood homes before a buyer decides which one to revisit.

01

Brentwood

Read two parcel tax bills side by side, verify a school assignment and check the walk to the Marsh Creek Trail.

 02

Pleasanton

Check what you can change near Main Street and which BART entrance fits your commute.

 03

Livermore

Look up an older home before planning a remodel, identify its water provider and compare the two ACE stations.

 04

Walnut Creek

Find the right permit desk, compare condo costs and choose between Walnut Creek and Pleasant Hill BART.

 05

Discovery Bay

Trace the route from a dock, find the office that handles permits and check flood information for the parcel.

 06

Oakley

Look up a project on the land next door, check space for a workshop and try the route to Big Break.
