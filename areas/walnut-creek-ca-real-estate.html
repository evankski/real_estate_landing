Buying or selling a home in Walnut Creek

Two listings can both say Walnut Creek and send you to different places for permit records. Before comparing prices, I’d establish who has the records and exactly what’s included in each property.

Review a Walnut Creek address

By Evan Karwowski · WR Properties · Updated November 3, 2026

Start with the parcel, then the building

Who keeps the permit history?

The city lists Saranap and parts of Walnut Heights among the places where a Walnut Creek mailing address may be outside city limits. Look up the parcel before requesting records. If the city lookup displays “UNIC,” ask Contra Costa County for the building records. This matters if you’re counting on a finished addition or making plans for another one. Walnut Creek zoning lookup · Building records by jurisdiction

For a condo, what does your monthly payment cover?

Request the HOA budget, reserve study, recent minutes and insurance summary. Check who maintains windows, balconies and plumbing, and whether the parking space you saw belongs to the unit. Those answers can make a difference between two listings with nearly identical asking prices and dues.

Choose the station entrance that fits your route

Walnut Creek station is on Ygnacio Valley Road. Pleasant Hill/Contra Costa Centre station is on Treat Boulevard and also has a Walnut Creek mailing address. Try the actual walk, bike ride or drive to the entrance you’d use. Walnut Creek station · Pleasant Hill/Contra Costa Centre station

Choosing Walnut Creek comparables

For a condo, I’d look in the same building first and compare floor, parking, outdoor space, dues and recent assessments. For a detached home near a city boundary, I’d check the jurisdiction and any records supporting added living space. I’ll identify the closest closed sales and explain where an adjustment needs evidence.
