Pleasant Hill, CA real estate guide

A listing that says “near Pleasant Hill BART” tells you something about the commute. It still leaves two questions to answer. How would you reach the platform, and which office keeps the records for this particular home?

Plan your Pleasant Hill home search

By Evan Karwowski · WR Properties

A Pleasant Hill address, a station and a city boundary

Start with the parcel if you plan to remodel.

Pleasant Hill and the nearby unincorporated Contra Costa Centre are separate jurisdictions. Look up the parcel before asking for permits for a studio or addition. The county’s directory links to CCMAP and identifies the planning and building offices for each jurisdiction. Check the parcel and permit jurisdiction.

Walk the last part of the station trip.

The Pleasant Hill/Contra Costa Centre station has a Treat Boulevard address and connects with buses and the Iron Horse Trail. On a weekday, walk or ride your proposed route to a specific station entrance. Count the crossings, consider the return trip after dark and compare that with the parking option. If buyers will be comparing your place with condos closer to the station, bring the HOA budget, parking assignment and an outline of what the dues cover. We can separate the value of the location from the cost of owning each home.
