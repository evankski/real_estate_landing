Plan your Dublin home search ↗

A closer look

What I would look at with you.

Add up the ownership costs.

Review taxes, any special assessments, HOA dues, insurance, and solar agreements. For a condo or townhome, understand what the association pays for and what you must cover.

Review condition and documentation.

Ask for inspection reports, permits for changes, and available builder warranties. A recent construction date does not replace a property inspection.

Test transit and driving options.

If BART or a particular commute is central to your plans, check the trip from the address, station access, parking, and the schedule you would actually use.

Questions to bring to a showing

  • What do the HOA’s reserves, insurance, and meeting minutes show?
  • Are there restrictions that affect your intended use?
  • How does this home compare with options in Pleasanton or Livermore?
