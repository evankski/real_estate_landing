Concord, CA real estate guide

Two Concord listings can advertise the same square footage and very different spaces. A converted garage deserves a closer look, especially if you’re counting on it for a bedroom or office.

Plan your Concord home search

By Evan Karwowski · WR Properties

What counts when you compare two Concord homes?

Trace a conversion back to the permit.

At a showing, note which rooms look like additions or conversions. Search by street address and permit type, then compare the scope and inspection status with the space you saw. If the records leave a gap, ask the seller for plans and the permit center for clarification before assigning the room the same value as original living space. City of Concord permit records.

Choose the station you would actually use.

Concord Station is on Oakland Avenue. North Concord/Martinez is on Port Chicago Highway. If BART is part of your week, try the route from the front door to your platform at your usual departure time. A harder crossing or a longer trip to parking can change which house you prefer. See the station maps for Concord and North Concord/Martinez.

Selling a home in Concord?

If you’re selling a Concord home with a conversion, pull the permit record and any final inspection paperwork before choosing comparables. I can show you how the pricing changes when we compare the original layout with homes that have documented additions.

Discuss your Concord sale

Every property is different. Confirm costs, condition, boundaries, school assignments, and permitted uses with the appropriate sources and professionals.
