A closer look

What I would look at with you.

Review improvements and permits.

Ask about additions, garage conversions, updated systems, and work done without permits. Have the appropriate professionals assess anything that needs further review.

Allow for repairs after closing.

Read the inspection reports and distinguish immediate repairs from longer-term maintenance. Get estimates when the cost could change your decision.

Check the route you will use.

Compare drive times, transit access, parking, and the regular trips from each address you are considering.

Questions to bring to a showing

  • Which inspection findings need specialist follow-up?
  • Does the home’s documented living area match the space shown?
  • What would you change before moving in?
