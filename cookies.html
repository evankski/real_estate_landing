Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Your tracking choice

Optional Meta Pixel tracking stays off until you select Allow tracking. Choosing Keep tracking off lets you browse the site and submit an inquiry without enabling that optional measurement. Use Cookie Settings in the footer to review your choice.

Browser storage used by this site

The site uses local storage to remember whether you allowed or rejected optional tracking. That preference remains until you change it or clear the site’s browser data.

Temporary session storage may be used to recognize a successful form submission and avoid counting the same inquiry event repeatedly. These temporary markers do not contain your name, contact details, budget, or message. Your form answers are not saved in browser storage by this site.

Optional Meta Pixel

If you allow tracking, Meta Pixel can measure page views and successful inquiries. Meta may use cookies and receive information about the page, event, browser, device, and network connection. This site does not intentionally include your form answers in Pixel event parameters.

If you later turn tracking off, the site revokes consent for further optional Pixel use. To remove cookies already set in your browser, use your browser’s privacy controls. Clearing this site’s stored data also removes its remembered preference.

Global Privacy Control

When your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, this site treats it as an opt-out and keeps optional tracking off, even if an earlier saved choice allowed it.

Forms and external websites

Submitting an inquiry sends the information to the configured contact-management service or through FormSubmit for email forwarding. Optional tracking consent is separate from submitting that request; you can send an inquiry with optional tracking off. Third-party services and websites you visit through links may use their own cookies or storage under their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, email evan@thewowski.com. Read the Privacy Notice for more about information submitted through the site.
